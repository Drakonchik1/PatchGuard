# Browser cache and download clutter

## Symptom
Low disk space partly driven by browser caches and Downloads growth.

## Safe checks
Identify which browser profiles are in use. Clear cache only — leave passwords and bookmarks intact.

## Recovery steps
1. In the browser settings, clear cached images/files for the last 30 days.
2. Move or delete large finished downloads from the Downloads folder.
3. Empty Recycle Bin and re-check free space.

## Avoid
Do not delete the entire `%LocalAppData%` browser profile folder.
