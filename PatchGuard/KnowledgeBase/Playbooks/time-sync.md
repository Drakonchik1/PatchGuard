# Windows time synchronization

## Symptom
Certificate errors, update auth failures, or odd log timestamps suggesting clock skew.

## Safe checks
Compare the PC clock to a phone clock. Prefer Settings → Time & language before registry time hacks.

## Recovery steps
1. Open Date & time settings and enable Set time automatically.
2. Click Sync now.
3. Re-run the failed update or site login.

## Avoid
Do not disable the Windows Time service permanently.
