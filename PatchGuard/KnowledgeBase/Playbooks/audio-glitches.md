# Audio crackle and dropout checks

## Symptom
Audio stuttering during games or calls while CPU/GPU load is elevated.

## Safe checks
Confirm exclusive-mode apps are not holding the device. Test with another output (headphones vs speakers).

## Recovery steps
1. Open Sound settings and set the correct output device as default.
2. Disable audio enhancements on that device.
3. Close the heaviest background apps and retest a short clip.

## Avoid
Do not uninstall inbox audio drivers as a first step.
