# Pending reboot after updates

## Symptom
Update history or policy state indicates a reboot is required to finish servicing.

## Safe checks
Save work, confirm no long installs are mid-flight, and prefer an orderly restart over holding the power button.

## Recovery steps
1. Close apps and restart Windows from the Start menu.
2. After login, re-run PatchGuard After Windows Update scenario.
3. If the pending flag remains, open Windows Update and check for a stuck installment.

## Avoid
Do not force power-off during `Configuring updates` screens.
