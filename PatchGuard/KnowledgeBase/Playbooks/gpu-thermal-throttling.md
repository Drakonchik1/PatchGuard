# GPU thermal and throttling checks

## Symptom
GPU temperature or load findings suggest thermal pressure during games or creative apps.

## Safe checks
Verify the finding refers to the discrete GPU, not an Intel iGPU. Confirm case fans spin and vents are clear before changing software settings.

## Recovery steps
1. Clean dust from GPU and case intake vents (power off first).
2. In GPU control panel, prefer a quieter balanced power profile for desktop work.
3. Re-test with a short gameplay session and compare Monitor readings.

## Avoid
Do not raise voltage or unlock BIOS power limits as a first response. Do not disable thermal shutdown protections.
